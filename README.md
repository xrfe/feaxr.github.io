## About Feaxr

**Feaxr LLC** is a technology company dedicated to applying cutting-edge artificial intelligence to scientific computing and engineering.

Our mission is to bridge AI with engineering by developing intelligent software that helps researchers, engineers, and innovators better understand, simulate, and optimize complex physical systems.

Rather than building AI for general-purpose use, we focus on applying AI where it can deliver measurable value—in scientific research, engineering analysis, and digital twin technologies.

---

# Our Vision

We believe the future of engineering lies in **intelligent Digital Twins**—virtual representations of physical systems that continuously assist with analysis, prediction, monitoring, and optimization.

By combining Artificial Intelligence with scientific computing and immersive visualization, Feaxr aims to make engineering tools more intuitive, interactive, and accessible.

Our long-term vision is to enable engineers to collaborate naturally with intelligent digital models, accelerating innovation across science and industry.

## 🔬 OpenBSA

### Open Battery Simulation Agent

OpenBSA is an open, web-based platform for lithium-ion battery simulation and analysis.

Designed for researchers, engineers, educators, and students, OpenBSA combines scientific simulation with an integrated Large Language Model (LLM) to create a more intuitive engineering workflow.

Instead of navigating complex simulation tools alone, users can interact with an AI assistant that helps explain models, configure simulations, interpret results, and accelerate learning.

Key focuses include:

- Web-based battery simulation
- Lithium-ion battery modeling
- AI-assisted simulation workflows
- Interactive visualization
- Education and research

## 🖨️ AI Digital Twins for Additive Manufacturing

### From a Photograph to Engineering Insight

This ongoing research project explores a new workflow for creating intelligent Digital Twins directly from real-world objects.

Using a smartphone, users capture one or more photographs of a physical object. AI-based 3D reconstruction techniques convert these images into a digital mesh, which is then prepared for engineering simulation.

By combining AI with high-performance scientific computing, the reconstructed model can be analyzed to predict manufacturing behavior, enabling engineers to evaluate a design before fabrication.

Current research focuses on:

- 📷 Image-to-3D reconstruction
- 🧩 Automatic mesh generation
- ⚡ Parallel finite element simulation
- 🔬 Residual stress prediction
- 🖨️ Digital Twins for additive manufacturing

Our long-term vision is to create an intelligent engineering assistant capable of transforming a simple photograph into a simulation-ready Digital Twin for design verification, manufacturing optimization, and engineering analysis.
